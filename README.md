
# 📊 Customer Behavior Analysis (SQL | Python | Power BI)

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes **customer shopping behavior** using transactional data to uncover **purchase patterns, customer segments, and revenue drivers**. It follows an end-to-end analytics workflow—covering **data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL-based insights, and interactive dashboarding**.

The objective is to enable **data-driven decision-making** by identifying high-value customers, product demand trends, and key factors influencing sales.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze **customer purchase behavior and spending patterns**
* Identify **high-value and low-value customer segments**
* Evaluate **impact of discounts, frequency, and demographics on revenue**
* Detect **top-performing categories and locations**
* Build **interactive dashboards for KPI monitoring**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Python** → Data cleaning, EDA (`pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`)
* **PostgreSQL** → Data transformation, aggregation, and business queries
* **Power BI** → Dashboarding, DAX measures, data modeling

---

## 📂 Dataset

* ~**4,000 customer transactions**
* Features include:

  * Demographics: age, gender, location
  * Purchase details: category, price, season
  * Behavior: frequency, previous purchases, subscription status
  * Feedback: review rating

---

## 🔧 Data Processing

* Handled **missing values and inconsistent data**
* Performed **feature engineering** (age groups, purchase frequency segmentation)
* Removed duplicates and standardized data types
* Prepared structured dataset for **SQL and BI analysis**

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

* **Total Revenue**
* **Average Order Value (AOV)**
* **Customer Count**
* **Average Rating**
* **Repeat Purchase Rate**

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 👤 Customer Behavior Analysis

* Segmented customers based on **purchase frequency and spending**
* Identified **high-value (VIP) vs low-value customers**
* Evaluated **repeat vs new customer behavior**

---

### 🛍️ Product & Sales Analysis

* Analyzed **sales by category and season**
* Identified **top-performing product segments**
* Compared **revenue vs demand (orders)**

---

### 💰 Discount & Pricing Analysis

* Measured **impact of discounts on revenue and order volume**
* Identified **discount-driven purchasing behavior**

---

### 🌍 Location & Demographics

* Evaluated **revenue distribution by location**
* Analyzed **spending patterns across age groups and gender**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features (Power BI)

* KPI cards (Revenue, AOV, Customers, Ratings)
* Filters for:

  * Gender
  * Subscription status
  * Location
  * Product category
  * Payment & shipping type
* Visuals:

  * Sales by category & season
  * Customer segmentation
  * Revenue by location
  * Purchase frequency analysis
  * Discount impact

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Top customer segments contribute a major share of total revenue**, indicating strong revenue concentration
* **Frequent buyers exhibit higher average spending**, highlighting retention opportunities
* **Discount-driven purchases increase order volume but may reduce AOV**
* **Certain categories dominate revenue despite lower order counts**, indicating premium pricing potential
* **Regional demand concentration enables targeted marketing strategies**

---

## 🚀 Business Impact

* Supports **customer targeting and retention strategies**
* Enables **inventory and demand planning**
* Helps optimize **pricing and discount strategies**
* Provides a scalable framework for **retail analytics and BI reporting**
